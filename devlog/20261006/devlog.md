# Zombie guts: now with a purpose

*October 6, 2026*

Yesterday's update left you with a pile of zombie guts and no good reason to keep them. The only thing you could do with them was eat them, and that is a bad reason.

Today they do something else.

## Throwing guts

If yer holding zombie guts when a zombie turns up, there is a new option in the fight: throw them.

The zombie stops to eat. It does not attack you for the turn you threw them, and then it is busy for three more turns. On the fourth turn it finishes the guts and goes back to trying to kill you.

If you throw more guts while it is already eating, the count goes back to three. It does not add up. A new zombie always starts out hungry for something other than guts.

So the loop is: holy water explodes a zombie and leaves guts, and the guts buy you time against the next one. Or you eat them. Yer call.
